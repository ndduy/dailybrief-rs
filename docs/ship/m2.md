## What shipped

- `harness::trajectory`: a typed `Event` over every stored `stream-json` line with a
  raw-keeping `Unknown`; turns folded by assistant message id; caps used against the
  configured limits; the retry chain (ADR 0012). Lines travel adapter → writer over a bounded
  channel and cannot be dropped.
- `/runs/{id}`: caps bar, retry chain, one row per turn; `/runs` with turns and wall clock;
  the day page's failed and running states carry the caps bar. `dailybrief run --max-turns`.
- Harness: token redaction before storage, `runs.error` scanned, lossy stdout reads, SIGKILL
  after grace and killed-run transcripts proven, a fourth publish after `fatal` refused.
- Retention: `dailybrief prune [--days] [--dry-run]`; one scheduler loop drives the 06:30 run
  and the 07:00 prune (ADR 0013).
- Web: `/run/status` from the lock holder; the manual cap counts runs; JWKS forced refetch
  throttled; HS256 confusion pinned by test.
- Core: one `(id, vector)` projection per ingest batch; 14-day title window; concurrent
  `fetch_sources` fetches once; the extractor is logged for ADR 0014.
- Ops: vendored, sha256-pinned installer with `curl`/`zstd` purged (527 MB image); licence
  lists kept identical by test; `harness.mcp_command` dropped; `DAILYBRIEF_IMAGE` rollback
  variable; `docs/runbook.md`; ADR 0015.

## `/ship` decision (2026-09-22)

**GO.** Three specialist reviews of a3372a6..44fec02: code-reviewer APPROVE (no Critical,
four Important), security-auditor no Critical or High (one Medium, five Low), test-engineer
two Critical test gaps plus Important items. Everything Critical, Important, Medium and the
cheap Lows were fixed before the decision, in two gated commits (9aaa7c7, c499c53):

- redaction on every stdout line and on the result's error text, not only the stderr tail;
- `errors[]` modelled, so `runs.error` carries the sentence the harness gives;
- the daily prune fires after a morning run that overran 07:00, and a directory that cannot
  be removed no longer stops retention (its rows stay, it is reported, the rest is pruned);

- the resolver's address filter is a tested function and the client ignores proxy variables;
- the caps bar is styled on the day page's failed and running states; the retry chain is
  read by time window; `allow_loopback` cannot come from the file; per-turn error counts;
  runbook notes on `DAILYBRIEF_IMAGE`.
